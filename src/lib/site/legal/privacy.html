Content

Source: you, public web pages and store endpoints

Social

What: plan, credit balance and ledger, Stripe customer and invoice IDs, billing address/VAT if given

Source: you, Stripe (we never see full card numbers)

Usage logs

Source: the Service

Moderation

What: for each screened prompt: verdict, category, scores, reason, acting user

Source: the Service

Source: certified verification provider

Technical

What: IP address, browser, device, pages, errors

Source: your device

We do not ask for special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR). Do not include them in prompts unless necessary.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We use this data solely to create your feega account and let you sign in to it.

We do not use Google user data for AI training, model improvement, advertising or to generate any content, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties except as needed to operate sign-in (our authentication and hosting provider) or where required by law.

feega’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke feega’s access at any time from your Google Account settings, and ask us to delete your data at support@feega.app.

8. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest; you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@feega.app. We answer within one month. You can complain to the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it) or your local authority.

9. Minors

The Service is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data of minors; if we learn of it, we delete the account. Age-restricted features require verified age.

10. Security

Encryption in transit, row-level access control per workspace, private storage buckets with signed links, hashed API keys, restricted use of privileged credentials, and provider security certifications. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and the authority of breaches as required by Art. 33–34 GDPR.
