title: Account
description: Signing in to Feega is optional.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Everything works without an account. Signing in connects Feega to your account, for the cloud features that come later.

Sign in from the Feega logo menu in the editor: **Sign in to Feega** opens your browser. You can cancel while it waits. **Sign out** clears the session.

With `npx feega`, the session is stored on your machine in `~/.config/feega/session.json`, readable only by you. Nothing else about your project is sent.
