---
title: Privacy
description: What stays on your machine and what is sent.
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

- Your code never leaves your machine. There is no cloud copy and no model.
- No account is needed.
- If you sign in, your sign-in goes to Feega.
- Anonymous usage counts (opens, edits, framework, styling system) are sent, with no code and no paths. Turn them off with [`feegaInspect.telemetry`](/more/settings/). They are also off when VS Code telemetry is off.
