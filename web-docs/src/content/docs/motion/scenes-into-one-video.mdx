Select two or more frames and pick **Combine into video**. They play in order, left to right, and export as one video. Editing a scene updates the video.

In Properties, a combined video lists its scenes:

- Drag to reorder.
- Set each scene's length.
- Pick a transition between scenes.
- Insert any frame as a new scene.

Embeds and share links of a combined video play every scene.

Storyboards (CLI `feega motion storyboard`, MCP `get_storyboard`, `write_storyboard`, `edit_storyboard_card`) are the agent-side way to plan scenes.
