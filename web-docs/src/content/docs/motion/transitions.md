Between the scenes of a [combined video](/motion/scenes-into-one-video/) you pick a transition in Properties.

**Unverified:** the list of available transitions. Shader effects, which agents can list with `list_shader_effects` (CLI `feega motion shaders`), are a separate system. Effects nodes also colour grade, remove a green screen, and crop or pad to a ratio like 9:16.
