A gallery of text animations with a hover preview: fade, slide, scale, blur, drop, spin and typewriter, per letter, word or line.

**Counter animation:** text counts from one number to another, with currency, percent and compact formats, decimals and easing.

Text itself (links, lists, gradients, underline) is covered in [Shapes, text and vectors](/canvas/shapes-text-vectors/).
