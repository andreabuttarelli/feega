A gallery of text animations with a hover preview: fade, slide, scale, blur, drop, spin and typewriter, per letter, word or line. They start where the In / Out section says: at the clip edge, or at the playhead.

A text layer has no animation of its own: it shows as drawn from its first frame. Its **Entrance** setting (Fade up) gives it the old soft fade-up as the clip starts.

**Counter animation:** text counts from one number to another, with currency, percent and compact formats, decimals and easing.

Text itself (links, lists, gradients, underline) is covered in [Shapes, text and vectors](/canvas/shapes-text-vectors/).
