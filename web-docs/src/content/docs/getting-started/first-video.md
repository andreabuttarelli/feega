---
title: Your first video
description: Animate a frame and export it.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

1. **Make a frame** at the video size you want. Video frames go up to 3840 × 2160.
2. **Switch to Animate.** A Design / Animate switch sits under every frame (key `E`). In Design, moving an element moves the whole animation. In Animate, a change is keyed at the playhead.
3. **Set keyframes**, or use **Animate to**: pick a property, type where it should end up, and the keyframes are created from where it is now. See [Keyframes and easing](/motion/keyframes-and-easing/).
4. **Add text motion** from the text animation gallery. See [Text animations](/motion/text-animations/).
5. **Play** with `Space`.
6. **Export.** MP4 (H.264 or H.265), ProRes, WebM with alpha, PNG sequence, GIF or APNG. See [Export](/motion/export/).

To make one video from several frames, see [Scenes into one video](/motion/scenes-into-one-video/).

You can also ask the [canvas chat](/ai/canvas-chat/) to build the animation.
