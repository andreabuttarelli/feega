title: Your first canvas
description: Make a frame, draw in it, share it.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. **Add a frame.** Pick a size: Phone, Tablet, Desktop, Presentation, Watch, Paper or Social. A "+" beside a frame adds a blank frame of the same size.
2. **Draw.** Tools are single keys: `R` rectangle, `O` ellipse, `T` text, `P` pen, `L` line, `Shift+L` arrow, `Shift+B` pencil. Full list in [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/generated/shortcuts/).
3. **Bring things in.** Paste from Figma, drop an SVG file, or drag an image into the frame. See [Figma and SVG](/integrations/figma-and-svg/).
4. **Start from a model.** The Models grid in the "+" menu (and the phone Insert sheet) adds an image, video or 3D node with a popular model already selected. Click a tile or drag it onto the canvas.
5. **Group and arrange.** `Cmd+G` groups, `Shift+A` adds auto layout, `Shift+S` wraps frames in a section.
6. **Comment.** Press `C`, then click anywhere to leave a comment.
7. **Share.** Copy a viewer link for a single frame from the export dialog. See [Sharing](/canvas/sharing/).

Free shapes, text, images and SVGs placed on the canvas are saved as elements, not frames. See [Frames and free elements](/canvas/frames-and-free-elements/).
