Rulers are off by default. Turn them on and, when you open a frame to edit it, two rulers appear inside its top and left edges, in pixels of the frame. Your choice is remembered. The labels adapt to the zoom: zoom out and the steps grow, zoom in and they get finer.

## In the editor

1. Press on the top ruler and drag down: a horizontal guide follows the pointer with its position in pixels.
2. Press on the left ruler and drag right for a vertical guide.
3. Release inside the frame to keep it. Layers snap to it while you move them.
4. Drag a guide to move it. Drag it back onto its ruler, or outside the frame, to delete it.
5. `Alt+Shift+R`, or **Rulers** in the top-left menu, shows or hides the rulers and the guides they hold.

Guides are saved with the frame, drawn in the editor, and never appear in an export. A frame holds up to 64.

## From an agent

The motion agent has two tools, also reachable through `edit` in MCP and `feega motion`:

| Tool | Input |
|---|---|
| `add_guide` | `axis` `vertical` or `horizontal`, `at` px from the left or top edge |
| `remove_guide` | `axis` and `at` for one guide, nothing to clear them all |

```bash
feega motion ask <nodeId> "Pull a vertical guide at 960 and a horizontal one at 540"
```

`get_motion_doc` lists them as `userGuides`.
