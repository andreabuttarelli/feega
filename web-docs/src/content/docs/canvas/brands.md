---
title: Brands panel
description: Switch between client brands and drag their material onto the canvas.
sidebar:
  order: 11
---

Open it from the left bar (palette icon, `Alt+3`).

- **Switch brands.** Search by name or slug. Pinned brands come first, then the brand linked to this project, then every other brand A–Z. The star pins or unpins a brand.
- **Status at a glance.** Each brand shows one line: accounts to reconnect, posts scheduled ahead, nothing scheduled, or no accounts connected. `feega brands` prints the same counts.
- **Drag onto the canvas.** Open a brand to see its kit: logo, colours, guidelines, social handles, store and website. Drag any of them onto the canvas to get the matching node.
- The panel opens on the project's own brand when it has one.
