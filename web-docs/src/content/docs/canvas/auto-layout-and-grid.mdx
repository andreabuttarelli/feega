A **Stack** is an element that lays its children out in a row or a column, like a flexbox `div`. It works inside a frame and free on the canvas. A Stack is the container clip with auto layout, read as a kind; every auto layout made before is a Stack ([ADR 0027](/architecture/adr/0027-stack-is-the-auto-layout-container/), [ADR 0008](/architecture/adr/0008-auto-layout-is-resolved-at-render/)).

## Wrap with stack

1. Select one or more elements: a text, a shape, an image, a component instance, inside a frame or free on the canvas.
2. Press `Shift+A`, or pick **Wrap with stack** from any of these:
   - right-click on the element on the canvas;
   - right-click on its row in **Layers**;
   - the **…** menu of the floating selection toolbar;
   - the **…** menu in the header of the right bar.
3. A new Stack holds the selection. Its direction and gap are guessed from where the elements were; text hugs its words and the Stack hugs its children.

Wrapping elements that already sit in a Stack nests the new Stack in their place. Wrapping free elements merges them into one free Stack element on the canvas, which grows and shrinks with its content.

**Remove stack** (`Alt+Shift+A`, same four menus, or the unwrap button next to the direction in the Stack section) unwraps: the children stay where the layout put them and the Stack goes.

## The Stack section

Select a Stack (its row in **Layers**, a click on it on the canvas, or `Esc` from one of its children) and the right bar reads top to bottom like Figma's auto layout panel:

1. The header: the name, then **Create component** and the **…** menu.
2. **Alignment**: left, centre, right; top, middle, bottom.
3. **Layout**: X / Y, rotation, rotate 90° and flip.
4. **Stack**, all chips with the label or an icon inside, no form rows:
   - **Direction**: vertical, horizontal, wrap, or grid, and the unwrap button. The sliders button in the section header opens the rarely used settings: **Canvas stacking** (first on top or last on top), **Strokes included**, stretch children, align text baselines (rows), space around or evenly, how wrapped lines spread, grid track sizes and fill order.
   - **W / H**: the size the Stack resolves to, with its mode in the same chip: **Fixed**, **Hug**, and for a child **Fill** or **%**. Typing a number makes it fixed; a typed size keeps the Stack's left and top edges where they are. Dragging a resize handle of a Stack on the canvas does the same on the side you drag: **Hug** becomes **Fixed** at the dragged size, the children re-lay out live while you drag, and the 3×3 grid places them inside the new box. Rotating a Stack turns it as one element around its centre. The mode menu also takes **Min** and **Max**: type `200`, `200px` or `50%` (a share of the parent Stack, inside its padding). A set min or max shows as a chip under W / H, with **×** to clear it.
   - A 3×3 grid sets the main and cross alignment at once, beside the **Gap** chip. Its menu switches the gap to **Auto**, which spreads the children apart (space between). On the canvas, drag the small handle in the middle of a gap or padding to change it.
   - **Padding**: a horizontal and a vertical chip. The per-side button opens four chips, left, top, right, bottom, each with its side's icon; pressing it again goes back to two, keeping the left and top values when the sides differ.
   - **Clip content**.
5. **Appearance**: opacity with the show / hide eye, corner radius (one value or per corner), blend.
6. **Fill**, **Stroke**, **Effects**: each with **+**.
7. **Selection colours**: every colour used inside the Stack; change one to change it everywhere in the Stack.
8. **Export**: **+** exports the selection.

## Stack children are flex items

The Stack places its children, like flexbox. Select a child and the right bar drops **X / Y**, frame alignment and constraints, and shows instead:

- **W / H** chips: the size in pixels, or the share when the mode is **%**; the mode menu picks **Fixed**, **Hug**, **Fill** (shares the space left; an agent can weight it, `widthGrow` 2 takes twice the space of 1) or **%** (a share of the Stack).
- **Min / max** width and height, in the same menu, in `px` or `%`.
- **Align self**: overrides the Stack's align for this child (start, center, end, stretch).
- **Absolute position**: takes the child out of the flow (Figma's *ignore auto layout*). X and Y come back, measured from the Stack's top-left corner.
- **Move earlier / later** and **Take out of the stack**.

On the canvas, dragging a child reorders it: a line shows where it lands between its siblings. Drop it on another Stack to move it there, or clearly outside the Stack to take it out. A child is never positioned freely inside its Stack. The arrow keys move it one place earlier or later along the Stack instead of nudging it. In **Layers**, dragging a child above or below another reorders it in that Stack, or moves it into the Stack of the row it lands on.

Fills, per-side strokes, corner radius, opacity, blend, shadows, blur and clip content are the usual sections.

In **Layers** a Stack has its own icon.

## Grid

Direction **Grid** gives fixed, fractional and hug columns and rows, spans, per-cell alignment and column-first fill.

## From an agent, MCP or CLI

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `wrap_in_stack` (motion) | wraps clips in a Stack |
| `set_stack` (motion) | direction, gap, padding, justify, align (`start`, `center`, `end`, `baseline`, `stretch`), wrap |
| `reorder_stack`, `leave_stack` (motion) | move a child, take a child out |
| `set_layout_item` (motion) | a child's sizing: `fixed`, `hug`, `fill` with `widthGrow`/`heightGrow` weight, `percent` with `widthPercent`/`heightPercent`; `alignSelf`; `absolute` |
| `remove_stack` (motion) | unwraps a Stack |
| `stack_elements` (canvas op) | wraps free elements on the canvas in one free Stack |

The old names `add_auto_layout`, `set_auto_layout`, `reorder_auto_layout` and `leave_auto_layout` still work until the next release.

## Export as code

A Stack exports as a `div` with `display: flex`, its direction, gap, padding, `justify-content` and `align-items` ([Export code](/canvas/export-code/)). A fill child exports as `flex: <weight> 1 0`, a percent child as `width: 50%`, its own align as `align-self`, and an absolute child as `position: absolute` inside a `position: relative` Stack.

`set_props` with `x` or `y` on a child in the flow is refused with a hint: reorder it, or make it absolute first.

## Arrange a selection

<DemoVideo name="arrange-selection" caption="Tidy up and stack four image nodes from the context menu, then the same ops from the CLI, and undo_last." />

1. Select two or more nodes: click one, hold `Shift` and click the others, or drag a box around them.
2. Right-click one of the selected nodes. With two or more selected, the menu lists **Align left / horizontal centres / right / top / vertical centres / bottom**, **Tidy up** and **Stack tiles**.
3. **Tidy up** untangles overlaps, **Stack tiles** puts the selection in a row or column, the align items line up the edges. Distribute is in the toolbar when three or more are selected.
4. `Cmd+Z` undoes the arrangement.

### From an agent, MCP or CLI

The same moves are canvas ops, free to run. Each takes the `canvasId` and the `nodeIds`; ids not on the canvas come back in `missing`.

| Op | Input | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `align_nodes` | `edge`: `left`, `center`, `right`, `top`, `middle`, `bottom` | the nodes moved |
| `distribute_nodes` | `axis`: `x` or `y`, 3 or more nodes | equal gaps, the outer two stay |
| `tidy_nodes` | none | overlaps untangled |
| `stack_nodes` | `gap?` | a row or a column |
| `undo_last` | `canvasId` | reverts your last write on the canvas, with the ones made in the same instant |

```bash
feega canvas $PROJECT align_nodes --input '{"canvasId":"<canvas>","nodeIds":["<a>","<b>","<c>"],"edge":"top"}'
feega canvas $PROJECT undo_last --input '{"canvasId":"<canvas>"}'
```

Over MCP the call is `canvas_tool({ project_id, op: "align_nodes", input })`. `undo_last` reverts what `canvas_events` recorded for the same actor: creates, deletes, data edits. `undo_last` does not undo moves or resizes: undo an arrangement with `move_node`, or revert it from [History](/canvas/history/).

## Related

- Tidy up: `Ctrl+Alt+T`. Distribute with an exact gap by typing it next to the distribute buttons.
- Layout guides take an auto count, min, max or centre alignment, a colour and an opacity; elements snap to their edges.
- Lock the aspect ratio of an element from the inspector; hold `Ctrl` to resize freely.
