---
title: Record the canvas
description: Capture the canvas or a selection as a video, with optional microphone narration.
sidebar:
  order: 12
---

**Record** captures what the canvas shows as a video, up to 60 seconds, and saves it on the canvas as a video asset.

1. Select what to record, or select nothing to record the whole view.
2. Tick **Mic** if you want narration. The browser asks for microphone access.
3. Click **Record** at the bottom right of the canvas. A clock counts up to 1:00.
4. Pan, zoom or edit while it records. Click **Stop**, or wait: it stops by itself at 60 seconds.

The video lands on the canvas as a normal video node, and a **Download** link appears next to the button. Where the browser supports it the file is an MP4 (H.264), otherwise WebM.

What is recorded is the canvas drawing: frames, layers and images. Interface chrome, the cursor and open panels are not part of it.
