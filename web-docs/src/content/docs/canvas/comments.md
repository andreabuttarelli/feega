---
title: Comments
description: Pin comments to the canvas, a node or a moment of a frame.
sidebar:
  order: 8
---

- Press `C`, or use the comment button, then click anywhere on the canvas or inside a frame. A comment on a frame follows it when you move it.
- Reply in threads and resolve them from the canvas menu.
- Edit or delete your own comments.
- Type `@` to mention a teammate. A dot shows when someone mentioned you in a canvas.
- Comments from teammates appear, change and disappear live.
- On a frame, **Comment here** pins a comment at the playhead; it shows on the timeline and a click jumps there.
- Write `@feega` in a comment and feega works on the node you pinned it to, then replies in the thread. This spends credits like any generation (**Unverified:** billing of comment-triggered runs).
