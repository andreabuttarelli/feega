[Ruixen UI](https://ruixen.com) is a React and Tailwind component library for shadcn projects. Its free, open-source components (MIT) ship with Feega as the **Ruixen** group of the component library: 155 components, from buttons, inputs and navbars to pricing tables, heroes and footers, each as editable layers.

## Insert a component

- **In the app**: on the canvas open **+** → **Components**, pick the **Ruixen** group, choose a component and press **Add to canvas**. Inside a composition, **Use in composition** places it there.
- **Agents**: Ruixen components are templates with ids `builtin:ruixen-<name>` (for example `builtin:ruixen-pricing-plans`). The motion and project agents list them with the other components and insert them by id; the API lists them at `GET /api/v1/motion/templates`.

## Animation

Many Ruixen components move: marquees, counters, reveals, scroll effects, WebGL backgrounds. Feega imports each one in its settled state, as a still of editable layers: transitions are skipped to their end before the import reads the page. Components drawn on a `<canvas>` are left out. The animation itself is not carried over; animate the layers on the canvas timeline if you need motion.

## What is and is not included

Only the components of Ruixen's open-source repository are included, pinned to one commit in the Feega repository. Ruixen Pro (the paid templates and premium components on pro.ruixen.com) is not fetched or stored. Of the 212 free components, 155 are included. The rest are listed with their reason in the pinned catalog:

- 5 need the Next.js runtime or files the registry does not ship.
- 10 do not come in as a component of their own: 2 only show after a click or a scroll, 8 are folded into the example around them.
- 26 use something the import cannot reproduce yet: 3D perspective (11), rotation (7), a `<canvas>` drawing (7), an image mask (1).
- 16 would insert an image as a grey placeholder.

## Known limits

- Animations arrive as a still frame.
- Text that mixes sizes in one line (a price and its `/month`) comes in at one size.
- Images load from raw.githubusercontent.com (Ruixen's repository) and third-party image hosts when you insert the component, so they need a connection.
- Logos in the examples are third-party trademarks, shipped as Ruixen ships them in its MIT repository.
