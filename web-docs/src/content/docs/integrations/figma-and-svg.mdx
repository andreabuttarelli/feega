## Paste from Figma

Copy layers in Figma and paste on the canvas.

- Shapes, text and groups land as free elements. Frames and sections land as frames. Pasting over a frame puts everything inside it.
- Rotation, stacked fills and strokes, gradients (including angular and diamond), and auto layout (direction, spacing, padding, alignment, sizing) come across.
- Small text keeps its real size, even in a very large frame.
- Component instances stay instances of one main component when the main is in the paste and the instance is untouched. An instance with overrides lands as a plain group that shows its own text, fills, hidden layers and swapped icons.
- Every text is kept, however small its size or long its paragraph.
- Layers keep their Figma names in the Layers panel.
- A short notice says how many things were approximated. **Details** lists them grouped by kind, and the notice closes by itself.

### Small text and component instances

<DemoVideo name="figma-text-instances" caption="Paste a Figma design with a main component and an instance, and a 12 px legal line in a 2880 px frame." />

- **Small text stays.** Text that is small next to its frame, such as a 12 px legal line in a 2880 px design, is kept at its size instead of being dropped.
- **Instances are linked.** When the main component is part of what you copied, its instances land as instances of one component. Select one: the inspector shows the component name, its text and fill fields, and **Swap**, **Reset overrides**, **Push to main** and **Detach**. Editing the main updates every instance.
- If the main component was not copied, an instance lands as a plain group named after the component.
- Overrides made on an instance in Figma (text, fills, hidden layers, swapped icons) are applied. That instance becomes a plain group, no longer linked to the main.

## Figma images

Figma puts only an image id on the clipboard, never the picture, so without a token images land as grey placeholders and the notice says so once. Add a Figma personal access token in Settings and the placeholders fill with the real pictures. Steps and troubleshooting are in [Connect Figma](/integrations/connect-figma/).

## SVG import

Drop or upload an SVG file to get editable vector layers, not a flat image. Gradients, rounded corners, clipping, text and cut-outs (holes) are kept. A pasted SVG keeps its holes too.
