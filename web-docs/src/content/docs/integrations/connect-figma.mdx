Pasting from Figma works without a connection: layers and text land as they are. Connecting Figma adds the images, and lets a Figma link import whole frames. Figma only puts an image's id on the clipboard, so Feega asks Figma for the picture with your account.

## Connect

1. Open **Settings** in the left rail, then **Profile** under Account.
2. In **Connections**, choose **Connect** next to **Figma**.
3. Sign in to Figma and approve access. You land back in Settings, with your Figma handle next to Figma.

The sign-in runs through Composio. Feega never sees or stores a Figma password or token.

Paste a Figma selection onto the canvas again. The grey placeholders fill with the real images in one step, and undo takes them back out.

## Disconnect

Settings, Profile, Connections: choose **Disconnect** next to Figma. Feega forgets the connection.

## Limits

- 40 images per request, 12 requests per minute per person.
- The connection only sees files your Figma account can open.
- Images are downloaded over https, up to 4 MB each.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Connect Figma in Settings to import images", after a paste | Figma is not connected | Connect it as above |
| "Figma refused the token…" after a paste | The connection lost access, or your account cannot open that file | Disconnect and connect again |
| "Too many Figma image requests…" | The rate limit was hit | Wait a minute and paste again |
| "Figma did not answer…" | Figma was down or unreachable | Paste again in a moment |
| "Connections are not set up on this server" | Self-hosted without `COMPOSIO_API_KEY` | See [Self-hosting](/self-hosting/) |

## From the CLI, MCP and API

Connecting happens in the app only. `feega figma-status <projectId>` and the MCP tool `figma_status` say whether Figma is connected. The endpoints behind it:

| Method | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/api/v1/projects/{projectId}/figma-connection` | `{ connected }` |
| `POST` | `/api/v1/projects/{projectId}/figma-images` | `{ fileKey, hashes }`; answers `409 no_token` when Figma is not connected, `422 token_rejected`, `429 rate_limited`, `502 unavailable` |
