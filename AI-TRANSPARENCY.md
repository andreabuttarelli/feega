# AI Transparency

> **Draft — to be reviewed by a lawyer before publication.**
> Last updated: 30 September 2026

feega is built on AI. This page explains, in the spirit of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the "AI Act"), which AI features exist, which models power them, how outputs are marked, how requests are moderated and what the limits are. It complements the [Terms of Service](./TERMS.md) (§7–§10), the [Acceptable Use Policy](./ACCEPTABLE-USE.md) and the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md).

## 1. Where AI is used

| Feature | What the AI does |
|---|---|
| Chat agent | a conversational AI assistant in the sidebar that reads and edits your canvas, creates nodes and runs generations on your instruction |
| External agents (MCP, API, CLI) | AI agents you connect act on your workspace with your API key; feega does not control those agents |
| Text generation | writes and rewrites captions, scripts, articles and other text nodes |
| Image and video generation | creates and edits images and video from prompts and reference media |
| Audio generation | voice-over, music and sound effects |
| Brand analysis | reads a brand's public website to suggest voice, palette and assets |
| Research | searches the web and public social posts to inform content |
| Moderation | screens every generation prompt before it reaches a model (§5) |

**You always know you are talking to an AI.** The chat agent is an AI system, not a person. Nothing in the Service is presented as a human reply.

## 2. Models and providers

- **Dynamic catalogue.** Text, image and video models come from a catalogue synced from OpenRouter, which routes each request to the model's provider (for example Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Black Forest Labs and others). The catalogue changes as models are released and withdrawn; the model and its provider are shown in the Service before you run a generation, and recorded in your usage log.
- **Recommended models.** For each medium the Service suggests a small set of models, chosen by quality, recency and price. You can pick any other model in the catalogue.
- **Video transport.** Some video and image models may be served through Kie.ai instead of OpenRouter.
- **Audio.** Voice, music and sound are generated by ElevenLabs.
- **Uncensored models.** Wiro serves a catalogue of uncensored image and video models, offered only inside NSFW mode, which is **not yet available**.
- **Moderation.** TypeSafe's Jev classifier and an AI reviewer reached through OpenRouter.

The full list of providers, with locations, is in [Sub-processors](./SUBPROCESSORS.md). feega does not train models, and does not use your content to train its own or third-party models.

## 3. Labelling of generated content

- **Machine-readable marking.** Images and videos generated by the Service carry an IPTC `DigitalSourceType` marker in embedded XMP metadata: `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` for fully generated media, `compositeWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia` for media that combine your material with generated elements. The marker is inserted without re-encoding the file. If marking fails for a technical reason, the file is still delivered unmarked rather than lost.
- **Signed Content Credentials (C2PA)** and **visible "AI-generated" labels** across the Service are **being rolled out**. In NSFW mode every result will be labelled as AI-generated, including in the downloaded file name.
- **Your duty.** Some platforms strip metadata on upload. When you publish a deepfake of real people, places or events, or AI text on matters of public interest without human editorial review, you must disclose that it is AI-generated (Terms §10).

## 4. Deepfakes and real people

- Sexual or nude content depicting a real, identifiable person is always refused.
- Deepfakes made to deceive, defame or harass are prohibited in every mode.
- Using a real person's likeness or voice requires their consent; do not present synthetic people or voices as real customers, employees or testimonials.
- In NSFW mode an additional check refuses prompts and references that identify a real person.

## 5. Automated moderation

Every generation prompt, including reference descriptions, is screened **before** it reaches a model:

1. **Classifier.** Jev scores the prompt against a fixed list of categories.
2. **AI review.** When the classifier is in doubt, an AI reviewer decides.
3. **Fail-closed.** If the review cannot run, the request is refused, not let through. In NSFW mode a classifier outage also refuses the request.

| Category | Standard mode | NSFW mode |
|---|---|---|
| Sexual content involving minors or apparent minors | refused | refused |
| Sexual content of a real, identifiable person | refused | refused |
| Non-consensual sexual content | refused | refused |
| Graphic violence and gore | refused | refused |
| Sexual content involving animals | refused | refused |
| Self-harm | refused | refused |
| Hate | refused | refused |
| Weapons and terrorism | refused | refused |
| Any other sexual, erotic or nude content | refused | allowed for fictional adults |

Each decision (stage, verdict, category, scores, reason, acting user) is logged. Moderation is automated and can be wrong in both directions: it refuses a request, it does not judge you.

## 7. Limitations

AI outputs can be wrong, invented ("hallucinated"), outdated, biased, offensive, or similar to existing works or other users' outputs. Text can state false facts with confidence; images and video can contain distorted details, text or anatomy. Review every output before you use, publish or advertise it.

## 8. Risk classification

- feega is a general-purpose content tool. It is not designed or offered for any high-risk use listed in Annex III of the AI Act (for example employment, credit, education, essential services, law enforcement, migration, justice), and such uses are prohibited by the Terms.
- The practices prohibited by Article 5 of the AI Act are excluded: feega does not use, and does not allow you to use it for, manipulative or exploitative techniques, social scoring, predictive policing by profiling, untargeted facial-image scraping, emotion recognition at work or school, sensitive biometric categorisation or real-time remote biometric identification.
- feega makes no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people.
