# AI generation moves to the left panel

The bottom bar pinned four AI generation buttons (text, image, video, sound) and the + menu
repeated them. They now live in a new left-rail panel, "AI generation" (Alt+8), listing every
AI node type. Click inserts at the canvas centre, drag drops where you release: same
`onCreate` path the bar used, reached through `insertRequest`.

The left rail is anchored at the top, under the top bar (was vertically centred).

Discarded: keeping the + menu Generate section with AI nodes. It now lists only non-AI nodes.
