# Terms: who answers for outputs

Borrowed from Wiro.ai's terms the clauses ours lacked: outputs are not
reviewed or endorsed before delivery; the user owns every downstream use,
including via MCP/API agents and automations (relevant because external
agents drive the canvas); human review before consequential decisions;
no professional advice; models can change. Prohibited uses gained:
competing product/model training on outputs, disinformation, biometric
surveillance, attacks and malware. Added a right-not-obligation to
monitor, so not policing everything is not read as a duty we failed.
