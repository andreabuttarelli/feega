# Video frames up to 4K

Doc sides were capped at 1920 x 1080 (plus a 1440 square) while the 2160p export preset already
existed, so a 4K frame could not be authored.

- `MAX_SIDE` 3840, `MAX_SHORT_SIDE` 2160 in `src/lib/motion/doc.ts`. Every size clamp
  (`custom-size`, `move-element`, `draw-create`, `set_canvas`) reads these two, so they follow.
- Stills keep their own 16384 range. The 1440 square exception stays and is now redundant.
- Export at 2160p of a 4K frame renders at native size (scale 1).
