# The feega mark in the app

The canvas top bar's main menu button showed a burger glyph; the app sidebar showed the word
"feega" alone. Both now carry the new mark (`FeegaMark.svelte`, four rotated lobes in
`--brand-orange`).

- Top bar: mark plus a small chevron-down in the same `burger-btn` button. Name, menu and keyboard
  behaviour are unchanged; the hit target grows from 28px to 36px wide.
- App sidebar: mark before the wordmark, linking to `/app`.
- Tone is an enum (`MarkTone.Brand` orange, `MarkTone.Current` inherits text colour). Orange is
  used on both themes; mono was not needed.
- Not touched: favicon, app icons, desktop icon, landing, OG images.
