# Outpaint (F1, 5)

`image_tool` gains `outpaint` {prompt, extend:{left,top,right,bottom}} (fractions of the picture).
Same engine as inpaint, no new model: `lib/outpaint.ts` plans the padded canvas and the mask
(white over the new strips); `edit-assets.paddedPng` composites the picture on a neutral grey
canvas; both are stored as assets and the new node carries `editImageRefId` (sent instead of the
wired picture) and `maskRefId`. UI: `OutpaintDialog` (four percentages + prompt).
Unverified against a real call: that ideogram fills a grey strip coherently.
