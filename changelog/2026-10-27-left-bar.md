# Left bar: panels only, clearer icons, Alt+digit shortcuts

**Before.** The rail mixed panel toggles with a link to Photo studio (a separate page).
Brands used a `building` icon nobody read as "brand"; tooltips had no shortcut and no
shortcut existed. Layers and Assets panels repeated the rail as a `SegmentedControl` tab
header. The right bar used `--grey-0`, darker than every other floating panel in dark mode.
The top-bar connection dot sat 3px from the pill's rounded edge.

**Now.**

- `shell-nav.ts`: Photo studio moves to a `menu` group, read by `BURGER_ENTRIES` only.
- Brands → `palette`, Influencers → `users-round`.
- `NavEntry.shortcut` (`Alt+1..4`, rail order); `panelForShortcut` maps a keydown to the
  panel; the project layout toggles it. Alt+digit was unused by canvas shortcuts.
- `CanvasLeftPanel` always shows a plain `<h3>` title; `onkind` removed.
- `RightBar` background → `--ui-panel-raised`; the dot gets 6px start margin, so its gap
  to the pill edge equals the chat icon's (9px).

Discarded: single-letter shortcuts (all taken by canvas tools).
