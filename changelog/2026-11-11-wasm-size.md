# Perf: engine wasm 4.4 MB → 2.8 MB

`packages/gpu-core` release profile: `lto = "fat"`, `codegen-units = 1`, `panic = "abort"`,
`strip = true` (the shipped file carried a 1.4 MB name section). On wasm32, vello builds without
`wgpu/default` (only `webgpu` + `wgsl`); native bins keep the defaults.

| | raw | gzip -9 | brotli 11 |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 4 435 154 | 1 007 853 | 685 244 |
| after (`s`) | 2 758 256 | 799 385 | 578 981 |
| `z` | 2 678 574 | 747 510 | 550 652 |

Kept `opt-level = "s"`: on the landings bench (uncapped frames, 3 interleaved rounds) `z` was
~15% slower on pan and drag; `s` matched or beat the old build on every gesture.

Discarded `wasm-opt`: `-O2`…`-O4`/`-Os`/`-Oz` shrink raw bytes 9% but grow gzip/brotli 5–6%
(transfer is what counts); `-O1` saves under 1%. Twiggy: no large separable chunk — fonts
(read-fonts + skrifa, ~620 KB pre-strip) are needed by every canvas with text.
