# Researcher and Sound sub-agents

Two more roster rows, each a round with its own model, tools and budget
share inside the turn:

- **Researcher** (Sonnet, 35%): opens a new-video turn with the research
  tools only (site, app, references, recreate_ui; no script writing).
  The Director round follows with the conversation it left.
- **Sound** (Sonnet, 10%): after the shot builder's first cut, a pass
  with music/beat tools only: lay music, mark beats, cut to them.

Costs are logged as `motion-researcher` / `motion-sound`.
Parallel shot builders: not built.
