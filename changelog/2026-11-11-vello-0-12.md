# Engine: vello 0.11 → 0.12

Re-vendored `packages/gpu-core/vendor/vello` and `vendor/vello_encoding` from the 0.12.0 crates and
re-applied our local patches (texture pool + bind-group cache in `wgpu_engine.rs`, the `glyphless` feature from #2008, doubled tile
buffer in `vello_encoding/src/config.rs`). Upstream changed only `scene.rs`, `util.rs` and the
encoding of blurred rounded rects: 0.12 adds `invert` to `draw_blurred_rounded_rect_in`, needed for
analytic inset shadows. Same wgpu (30).

No behaviour change intended. `cache.rs a_cached_frame_draws_the_same_pixels_as_the_expanded_one`
(GPU, `--features gpu`) fails identically on `dev` before the bump (worst diff 89).
