# View menu and Studio on the rail

Issue #1521, spec `docs/specs/editor-modes.md`.

- Outlines, pixel grid, guides, rulers and pivot were keys only (rulers also in the burger).
  `ViewMenu.svelte` in the top bar lists them from `VIEW_ITEMS` (`view-menu.svelte.ts`).
- The view toggles moved from a local `$state` in `GpuFlow.svelte` to the shared `frameViews`,
  so keys and menu flip one state. Rulers stay a persisted preference.
- Studio moved from the `menu` group to `workbench` in `shell-nav.ts`: the desktop rail shows it,
  the burger still lists it.
