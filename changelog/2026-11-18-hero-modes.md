# The landing hero says both ways to run Feega and what each costs

Isolated first-time readers (Haiku and Sonnet, screenshot of the fold only) could not tell how the
web prompt, the Download button and `npx feega` differed, nor whether Feega costs money or needs an
account: 0 of 4 answered it from the fold. The line under the prompt only said "or run it locally".

It now has two lines, "On your computer" and "In the cloud", with the price read from
`pricing/plan.ts` so it cannot drift from the pricing page. After the change 4 of 4 readers named
both modes, the price and the account rule. The local line then gained "Mac app or" and the agent
names, because readers still asked what Download installs and which AI "the AI you already have"
meant; that last tweak was not re-tested.
