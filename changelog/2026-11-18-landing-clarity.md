# The landing says what Feega does before it shows off

Six isolated first-time readers (Haiku and Sonnet, the whole page as screenshots, no other
context) rated the landing 2/5 for clarity. All six named the agent cursors as the thing to cut:
they covered the copy on every section and nobody could tell whether they were features or
decoration. Other common notes: "agents" never explained, "No lock-ins" and "Made for humans and
agents" meaningless, the credit calculator the moment they wanted to leave.

- `LiveAgents`: the crew builds only what is on screen at load, then leaves. Everything below is
  in place from the start (`actOf` in `live-agents.ts`, one rule: opening, resident, still). The
  footer wordmark keeps its standing crew. The idle tweaks across `main` are gone.
- Hero lead now names the action: paste a site or open a repo, edit by hand or with an AI
  assistant, export a video or send the code back.
- "Let your agents do the work" gains one sentence saying what an agent is.
- Cut: the "No lock-ins" proof pair and the "Made for humans and agents" card (`Card.Agents`).
- The credit estimator folds behind "Estimate what you'd use".

Re-read by six new readers: every one now describes the product the same, concrete way ("paste
your site, edit the layers, get the code back"). Clarity still averages about 2/5: they still
see three products (Code, Motion, AI), too many start buttons, no demo, and two FAQ blocks.

Kept on purpose: the headline, which carries the SEO title and OG alt.
