# 120 fps

`FRAME_RATES` gains 120. The same list feeds the doc (`fps`), the export settings and
`setFrameRate`, so a doc can run at 120 and the dialog, CLI and MCP offer it.

- Frame ceiling follows `FASTEST_RATE`: 180 s x 120 = 21600 frames. Edge and junction caps
  (`FASTEST_RATE * 2`) double with it.
- Browser export stops at 60 fps (`BROWSER_MAX_FPS`, `FarmReason.FrameRate`): 120 goes to the farm,
  on the whole-video route like 25 and 50 fps. H.265 at 120 is already refused there.
- Checked: dialog lists 120 and says it renders on our servers (`export-fps-120.spec.ts`).
  No farm render was run.
