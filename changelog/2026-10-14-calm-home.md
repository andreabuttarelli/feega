# A calm home: hero, recents, one prompt

## Why

The user found `/app` "too full, monotonous, no structure to give content importance": six
equal-weight rows (prompt hero, videos grid, component strip with two filter rows, posters strip,
projects grid, gallery strip), four different "start" paths, and the account links taking half
the sidebar. Reference: a competitor home with a greeting, a recents list and one prompt.

## What

- `/app` is three things: `HomeHero` (slogan + subline over a looping backdrop), recents as a
  compact list (thumb · name · project · time) and a sticky prompt dock with three suggestions.
  Top right: `new canvas` (primary, the existing `?/project` action) and `import` (files into the
  prompt's attachments).
- The catalogue moved to `/app/templates` (`ComponentShelf`, `PosterShelf`, gallery strip); its
  forms still post to `/app?/fromTemplate`. Sidebar: `New video` entry removed, `Templates` added.
- `?/video` and `?/fromTemplate` now open a **new canvas** per start (`CanvasPick.New` in
  `startMotion`), named after the brief or template, instead of piling every frame on the shared
  `Motion` canvas. The template is validated before the canvas is created, so an unknown id
  creates nothing. Other callers keep `CanvasPick.Motion` by default.
- Sidebar foot: avatar + name + email is a button opening the account menu (settings, billing
  with balance, changelog, report, tour, legal, theme, sign out). Esc and focus leaving close it.
- Hero copy lives in `src/lib/home-hero.ts`. Backdrop: `static/home/hero-flow.mp4`, cut from the
  `builtin:flow-lines` preview render (first 2.6 s, ping-ponged so it loops; the original ends on
  "go with the flow" text). It's a `<video>`, not WebGL: zero GL contexts. The poster `<img>`
  paints first; the clip mounts on idle, never under reduced motion or Save-Data, and pauses when
  the tab hides or the hero leaves the viewport (`backdropPlays`, tested).

## Discarded

- A live shader/player canvas for the backdrop: a WebGL context on the home for decoration costs
  more than a 125 KB loop and buys nothing visible at 50% opacity under a scrim.
- Recents as a grid of cards: the list is what lets the hero and the prompt breathe.
