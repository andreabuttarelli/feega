# Frame edits as ops, rebased on the head

Before: a frame was saved as a whole doc with an optimistic version. A second editor got a 409
and lost its edit; the motion agent, on conflict, re-saved its whole doc at the new head and erased
whatever the person typed during the turn.

Now edits are typed ops (`doc-ops.ts`, derived with `diffDoc`), and `commitOps` re-applies them on
the latest head: different clips/props commute, same field is last-writer-wins, delete beats edit.
The editor gets an `ops` action; the agent turn and MCP `runMotionTool` go through `commitDoc`.
The whole-doc `save` action stays as fallback. ADR 0007 has the reasons; no migration, no op table
(the ops between two revisions are their diff). Discarded: a CRDT library — strings in a frame are
short, last write wins on a field.
