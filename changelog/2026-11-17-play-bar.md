# Play screen: one bar

The prototype player had a bare title, grey text buttons and two cramped segmented bars
(modes, widths) plus a raw px field, on a hard-coded black backdrop. It ignored the theme.

Now `PrototypePlayer.svelte` has one 48px bar on shell primitives (`IconButton`, `Menu*`,
`Hint`, `Kbd`) in three zones: frame name / flow menu; device icons + width field + views
menu; restart, zoom menu, full screen, close. Stage and bar use `--ui-*` tokens, so light and
dark both work. The frame gets `md` radius and the float shadow only when narrower than the
stage. Handles turn accent on hover and drag, with a live width label.

Device presets and the `1/2/3` keys live in one table, `src/lib/motion/prototype/device.ts`.
Full screen (`F`) hides the bar after 2 s idle.

Tooltips and menus portal to `body` at `z-50`, under the player's `z-index: 1000`; the player
lifts them with a scoped `:global` rule rather than changing the shared primitives.

Kept: viewport model, per-frame memory, all test ids (`viewport-<width>`, `viewport-window`
now live in the views menu, so the parity spec opens `viewport-menu` first). No share button:
the player receives no share URL; Copy play link stays in Properties.
