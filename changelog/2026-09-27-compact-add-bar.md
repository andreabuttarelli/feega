# Compact add bar

The bar listed every addable node. Now: upload, `CANVAS_BAR_MAIN`
(the generative mediums), and a grid panel with `CANVAS_BAR_MORE`
(the rest of `CANVAS_ADD_BAR`). Number shortcuts keep the
`CANVAS_ADD_BAR` order, shown in each title via `slot()`. Bar sits 8px
from the bottom, FloatingRail 8px from the left.
