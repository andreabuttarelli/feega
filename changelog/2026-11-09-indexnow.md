# IndexNow after landing deploys

Search engines learned about new landing pages only by crawling. The deploy workflow now runs
`landing/scripts/indexnow.ts` after a `feega-landing` production deploy: it reads
`https://feega.app/sitemap.xml` and POSTs every URL to `api.indexnow.org` (batches of 10000). The key
is public by design and served at `landing/static/<key>.txt`. A non-2xx response fails the step.

Discarded: a `deployment_status` workflow (the deploy is already synchronous in `deploy.yml`, so a
step after it needs no waiting).
