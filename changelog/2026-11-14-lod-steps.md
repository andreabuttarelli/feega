# Zoom-out levels a half doubling apart, never below screen density

Report: a slight zoom-out turned the canvas grainy.

Cause: `Tiles::step_for` picked the pyramid level with `round(log2(dpr/zoom))`,
levels a full doubling apart. Between 71% and 100% of a level's density it
rounded to the coarser one: on a DPR 2 screen at 60–70% zoom each texel covered
1.2–1.4 device pixels.

Fix: levels every √2 (`STEPS_PER_LEVEL` 4 → 2, pyramid gains 2/6 below top),
and `floor` instead of `round`, so the drawn level is always ≥ 1 texel per
device pixel and at most √2 oversampled. Sharp level keeps its 25% share.

Also: `SCREENS_KEPT` 12 → 16, so on a retina desktop the 384 MB tile budget
binds (1,456 tiles) instead of the screen cap (1,152); fewer seen frames are
evicted.

Discarded: rastering every 2^(1/4) step on demand — re-bakes on every wheel
tick.
