# Audio docs (ElevenLabs)

`web-docs/src/content/docs/audio/`: overview plus one page per operation (text to speech, voice
changer, dubbing, music, sound effects, voice isolation), voices, and motion voice-over/music.
Traced from `audio-operations.ts`, `audio-run.ts`, `AudioControls`, `VoiceStudio`, `SoundDialog`,
`motion-tools.ts` and the MCP descriptions. Prices are the `usdPerUnit` table times 120 credits/USD.

No video tutorials yet: recording needs the ElevenLabs key and the Supabase service role, not
available to the agent. Pages have no `DemoVideo` until the clips exist.
