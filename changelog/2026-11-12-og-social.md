# Social previews: one image and video for the landing, the blog and the app

Previews still showed the old logo. The landing used a 4.9 MB `og.gif`, the blog a text card
(`og-default.png`), the app's public pages their own `og.png` with the alt text "An AI that runs
your social media", and the app root, login and share links nothing at all.

`landing/scripts/og/` now draws the preview. `scene.ts` builds every frame of an isometric canvas
from the landing's `iso/` helpers, the mark geometry and the ASCII field, tinted orange to warm
grey instead of the component's rainbow, which has no tint option. The text column holds the
lockup and the hero headline, `HERO_HEADLINE` in `home.ts`, which `Hero.svelte` reads too.
`render.ts` captures each frame in Chrome at twice the size, downsamples with lanczos and encodes
H.264. Run it from `landing/` with `npx vite-node scripts/og/render.ts [outDir] [filter]`. It
writes `static/og.png` and `static/og.mp4` (1200×630, a 10 s loop whose last frame is the image),
plus 16:9 and square cuts and a scene-only loop for native posts.

The files live once, in `landing/static/`. The blog and the app link them by absolute
`https://feega.app/` URLs: one source, one cache, no copy in each deploy.

- Landing: `OG` in `seo.ts`, read by the layout, checked by `seo.test.ts`.
- Blog: `SOCIAL_PREVIEW` in `site.ts`. Home and tag pages get the image and the video. A post keeps
  its cover and gets no video, because Discord would play the generic clip instead of showing the
  cover. `test/social-preview.test.mjs` checks the built HTML in CI.
- App: `SocialPreview.svelte` in the root layout sets the default once, using the landing home's
  title and description. A page that returns `socialImage` from its load overrides it, as the
  gallery does with its poster. The duplicates in `(public)` and `static/og.png` are gone.
  `social-preview.test.ts` pins the copy and URLs to the landing's.

Three compositions were drawn as stills first. The user picked the split one: text left, scene
right. The video is not a `twitter:player`: X reads no `og:video`, and a player card needs an
iframe page.
