# Landing: social preview image and video, rebuilt from source

The link preview showed the old logo: `og.gif` (4.9 MB, above most crawlers' limits) as
`og:image`, plus an `og.mp4` and an `og.png` with no source in the repo. The app's public layout
used another `og.png` with the alt text "An AI that runs your social media".

The preview is now drawn by `landing/scripts/og/`: `scene.ts` builds each frame of an isometric
canvas scene with the landing's `iso/` helpers and the shared mark geometry, and `render.ts`
captures it frame by frame in Chrome and encodes it with ffmpeg. Run it from `landing/` with
`npx vite-node scripts/og/render.ts [outDir] [filter]`. It writes `static/og.png` (1200×630,
palette PNG) and `static/og.mp4` (1200×630, 10 s loop). It also writes 16:9 and square stills, a
1920×1080, a 1080×1080 and a scene-only cut to `~/Documents/feega-videos/og/` for native posting.

The image carries the lockup and one line, "An open-source canvas to design, animate and
generate.", taken from the hero copy. The text sits in the centre so a square crop keeps it.
`OG` in `seo.ts` declares the URLs, sizes and alt text. The layout reads it, and `seo.test.ts`
checks that the files exist with those sizes.

`twitter:card` stays `summary_large_image`. X ignores `og:video`, and `twitter:player` needs an
iframe player page. The GIF is gone: a PNG is smaller, and the video covers the motion.
