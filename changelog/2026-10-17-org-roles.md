# Organisation roles: owner, admin, member, viewer

Roles were `owner | admin | member` with nothing reading them except
"owner sees billing"; `org_isolation` let any member write every row,
members and invites included.

`src/lib/org-roles.ts` is the one permissions table (`can(role,
action)`, `assignableRoles`). `viewer` is new: reads, never writes
(Figma's free View seat). Proposal and Figma sources:
~/Documents/feega-videos/orgs-teams/FIGMA-TEAMS.md.

Enforced:
- RLS (`20261017130000_org_roles.sql`): every `org_isolation` policy on
  a table with `org_id` (except `canvas_events`, presence) splits into
  read for members and insert/update/delete for editors
  (`auth_editor_org_ids()`). `orgs_members`: admins update/remove,
  never the owner row; anyone deletes their own non-owner row (leave).
  `orgs_invites`: admins only. `transfer_org_ownership(org, to)` swaps
  owner/admin atomically, owner only.
- API/MCP: `resolveJwt` sets `writeAllowed` from `can(role, Edit)`.

Known gaps: tables whose policies are not named `org_isolation` (e.g.
`influencers`) and the `canvas-assets` storage policies still let a
viewer write; the canvas UI does not yet hide edit controls for viewers.
