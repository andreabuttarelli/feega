# Stack words template

Hand-made template from a reference: portrait cards dealt in from the left, one per beat, each
landing as the large centred hero while the older ones shrink 16%, step left by a pitch (overlap
field) and take a fixed vertical offset, so the stack reads as cards on a table. Two words (shared
word entrance) land above and below the hero once it settles; everything slides off left for the
loop. Expo in-out only, no overshoot. Pure function of t, model tested first; 0 jolts on 16:9.
