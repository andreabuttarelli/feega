# Canvas: a hugging container grows and pushes its siblings, no page relayout

After #2195 a hide or resize under a container that hugs its content still laid out the whole
landing page: the walk up only stopped at fixed-size containers, and the page root hugs.

- `laidNear` (`fast-patch.ts`): lays out the innermost auto-layout container around the edited
  clip, then walks up. When a container's box changed only along its parent's main axis, and the
  parent flows in one line from the start (`justify: start`, no wrap, not a grid), the siblings
  after it and their subtrees are shifted by the size difference, and a hugging parent grows by
  it (its centre moves by half). The walk stops at the first unchanged box or at the root.
  Anything else (cross-axis change, a filling or absolute sibling, centred or spaced flow) falls
  back to the whole root tree.
- Guard: landings parity (1617 edits, 1e-5 of the page) 0 mismatches; patch vs full build 0
  mismatches. Two unit tests now compare within 1e-6 instead of exactly: shifting adds the delta
  through a different float path (`378.00000000000006`).
