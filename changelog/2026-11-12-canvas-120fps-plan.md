# Canvas 120 fps handoff plan

`docs/specs/canvas-120fps-plan.md` records the bar, how to measure it, what is merged, which
branches are unmerged and the work packages in order, so a session on an idle machine can carry
the engine work. Benchmarks on the first machine were invalid for most of 2026-11-12: load stayed
between 10 and 86 while other agents ran.
