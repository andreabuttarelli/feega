# Terms: explicit indemnity for likeness claims

The indemnity in TERMS.md §17 was one generic line. A generated face can
resemble a real person, and a user can upload someone's photo: the claim
lands on us. The clause now names likeness, voice and personality rights,
covers resemblance "whether or not intended", includes legal fees, and
gives us control of the defence. Consumer carve-out kept (fault-based,
within mandatory law) because a blanket consumer indemnity risks being an
unfair term under Codice del Consumo art. 33. Modelled on the standard
clause used by generation platforms such as Wiro.ai. Lawyer to confirm.
