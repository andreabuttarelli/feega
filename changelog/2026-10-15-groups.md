# Groups: on the canvas and inside frames

Canvas: ⌘G on 2+ free elements (`groupElements`, `server/motion/element-groups.ts`) builds one
chromeless frame of kind `group` over their union box; each clip keeps its canvas place and size
(`clipCentre`, element size re-expressed in the group's pixels). Sources are soft-deleted; undo is
`node.create` + `node.delete`s. ⇧⌘G (`ungroupElement`) makes one element per clip at its box. A
group opens only on double-click (`frameChrome.opening`, `openingFor`), so a click selects it
whole; a dropped group moves all its clips into a frame (`moveFree`, one write).

Frames: ⌘G is precompose named "Group N" (`groupClips`); ⇧⌘G (`ungroupClip`) puts the comp's
clips back at the same time and carries the group's x/y offset. A scaled, rotated or animated
group is refused rather than flattened wrong. Motion tool `ungroup`; MCP canvas ops
`group_elements`, `ungroup_element`.

Layers: precomp rows are named by their comp and fold (`foldable`, keyed by row key).
`elementName` names shapes Box / Ellipse / Path in Layers and on new element nodes.

Not done: rotating a canvas group (canvas nodes do not rotate), frames inside groups,
double-click into a group inside a frame (a `DRILL` row, owned by the drill-in work).
