# Alternatives section on the landing

Twelve articles under `/alternatives` (landing), modelled on dusk.bot's
series: roundups, use cases and single-tool pages, all argued from
"design in your codebase". `/compare` stays the 1:1 Feega-vs-X pages.

Each article is data (`landing/src/lib/site/alternatives/`): a verdict
lead, pick-them / pick-Feega, free sections (tables built with `table()`),
mistakes, questions (FAQ JSON-LD), numbered sources and a checked date.
Facts come from the vendors' official pages read on 2026-11-18.

Discarded: deriving the pages from `/compare` data (motion-centric rows),
and including ChatGPT Sites (official pages answered 403; no source).
