Making a piece of visual content today means a relay race between tools. You sketch in one, animate in another, generate an image somewhere else, then export, re-import and lose the thread every time you hand something over.

Feega is our answer: **one canvas for design, motion and AI.**

## One canvas

Everything lives on the same infinite canvas. Frames you design, scenes you animate and media you generate sit side by side, so you can see the whole piece at once and move material between its parts without exporting anything.

<DemoVideo name="combine-scenes" caption="Scenes combined on the canvas." />

## Design, motion and AI together

Design and motion are not two apps glued together. A frame you lay out is the same frame you animate, and the timeline works on the elements you already have.

AI is part of the canvas, not a separate destination. You can ask for an image, a video or a change from the chat beside your work, and the result lands on the canvas where you can keep editing it by hand.

## Open source

Feega is open source. You can read how it works, follow what we build, open an issue or send a pull request. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/andreabuttarelli/feega).

## Try it

One command gets you started:

```bash
npx feega
```

From there, the [docs](https://docs.feega.app) walk through the canvas, motion and the AI tools. If you want to talk to us, or show what you made, join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/PUp37DG6vr).

We are building Feega in public, and this blog is where we will write about it.
