# DMCA and copyright policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

feega respects intellectual property and expects its users to do the same. This policy explains how to report copyright infringement on the Service, how affected users can respond, and how we treat repeat infringers. It is part of our [Terms](https://feega.app/terms) (§12A).

## Sending a notice

Use the report form at [feega.app/report](https://feega.app/report) and choose "Copyright infringement (DMCA)", or email the agent. Under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3) a notice must include:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
2. The URL or other location of the infringing material on feega (for example a shared canvas link).
3. Your name, postal address, email and, optionally, phone number.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We confirm receipt by email. Incomplete notices may be rejected.

## What happens next

When a notice is valid we remove or disable the material (the canvas node is hidden and any share link is revoked), and we email the user who posted it with a copy of the notice, the reasons for our decision, and a link to send a counter-notice.

## Counter-notice

If you believe your material was removed by mistake or misidentification, use the counter-notice link in our email. Under 17 U.S.C. §512(g)(3) it must include your name, postal address and email, identification of the removed material and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification, your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, outside the United States, any judicial district in which feega may be found) and to accept service of process from the claimant, and your signature.

We forward the counter-notice to the claimant. We restore the material no sooner than 10 and no later than 14 business days after receiving it, unless the claimant tells us they have filed a court action to restrain you.

## Repeat infringers

Each upheld notice adds a strike to the account that posted the material:

| Strikes | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Warning |
| 2 | Account suspended for 30 days |
| 3 or more | Account terminated |

A restored item removes its strike.

## False claims

Under 17 U.S.C. §512(f), anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees.

## Other reports

Illegal content under EU law, likeness or voice used without consent, and child sexual abuse material are reported through the same form; see [Terms §12](https://feega.app/terms).
