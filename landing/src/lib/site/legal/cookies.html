Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy lists the cookies and similar storage Feega uses, under Art. 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 and the Garante’s guidelines of 10 June 2021. The Feega extension and npx feega run on your machine and set no cookies. How we process personal data is in the Privacy Policy.

No tracker runs on localhost, preview hosts or for Feega staff.

1. This website (feega.app landing)

The marketing site sets no cookies and writes nothing to your device. It loads one script, Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views and clicks on key buttons without cookies. Visitors are told apart only by a hash of the request, discarded after 24 hours. It needs no banner.

Purpose: aggregate page views and button clicks

4. Consent

The banner has equal Accept and Reject buttons. Accept enables sections 3 for analytics and marketing; Reject keeps them off. Your choice is stored in feega_consent for 180 days, then asked again.

5. Managing or withdrawing consent

Click “Cookie preferences” in the footer of the web app to reopen the banner and change your choice at any time. Withdrawing consent reloads the page and stops the trackers. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; blocking essential cookies prevents you from signing in.
