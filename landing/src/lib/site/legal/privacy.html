Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the feega.app website, the optional account and donations (the “Site”). It follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003. The Feega extension and npx feega run on your machine, send no code to us and use no AI.

1. Controller

Andrea Buttarelli, Italy, VAT no. 16090491008.
Contact: support@feega.app

2. Data we collect, why, and legal basis

Account

What: name, email address, profile picture, from your Google or GitHub sign-in

Why: to create your account and sign you in

Legal basis: Contract — Art. 6(1)(b)

Donations

What: name, email address, amount, country; payment and billing details are handled by Stripe, and we never see full card numbers

Why: to process the donation, issue invoices or receipts and keep accounting records

Legal basis: Contract; legal obligation — Art. 6(1)(b), (c)

Support emails

What: your address and what you write to us

Why: to answer you

Legal basis: Legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(f)

Site analytics

What: aggregate page views through Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies

Why: to understand how the Site is used

Legal basis: Legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(f)

We do not collect your code, and we do not ask for special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR). We do not sell personal data and make no decision based solely on automated processing (Art. 22).

Google and GitHub sign-in

From Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture; from GitHub the equivalent profile data. We use them solely to authenticate you. We do not use them for advertising or AI training, we do not sell them, and we share them only with the providers that operate sign-in and hosting, or where the law requires.

Feega’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access from your Google or GitHub account settings and ask us to delete your data at support@feega.app.

Some providers operate outside the EEA, mainly in the United States. Transfers rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified). Ask us for a copy.

Kept: until you close it; deleted within 30 days of closure

Kept: 10 years (Italian tax law)

Support emails

Kept: as long as needed to answer you

6. Cookies

The Site sets no cookies for analytics and needs no consent banner. Cookies and storage used by the web app are listed in the Cookie Policy.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@feega.app; we answer within one month. You can complain to the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it) or to your local authority.

8. Children

Feega is for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect data of younger people; if we learn of it, we delete it.
