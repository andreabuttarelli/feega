feega for Mac. The full feega app in a native window: sign in with email, Google or GitHub, and
work on your canvases. Open `.feega` files locally with no account (File → Open Local File…).

- Apple silicon: `feega-<version>-mac-arm64.dmg`. Intel: `feega-<version>-mac-x64.dmg`.
- The app is not notarised by Apple. macOS asks you to confirm it once:
  [install guide](https://docs.feega.app/desktop/install-macos/).
- Verify your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- The app tells you when a newer release is out.
