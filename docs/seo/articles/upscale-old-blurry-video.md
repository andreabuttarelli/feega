# Upscale old or blurry video with AI

AI upscaling makes low-resolution and slightly soft video sharper by rebuilding fine detail at a
higher resolution. It helps most with clean footage that is simply small; it helps least with
motion blur, heavy noise or damaged film.

## What AI upscaling fixes

- **Small exports**: a 480p or 720p clip that looks soft on a large screen.
- **Mild softness** from scaling or light compression.
- **AI-generated clips** rendered at low resolution to save cost.

## What it does not fix

- **Motion blur** from a slow shutter: the detail was never recorded.
- **Heavy noise and blocky compression**: they get sharper too. Denoise first.
- **Black-and-white or faded colour**: upscaling does not colourise or regrade.
- **Shaky footage**: no stabilisation.

## Getting the best result

1. Start from the best copy you have; re-encoded copies lose detail each time.
2. Trim to the part you need (feega takes up to 20 s per clip).
3. Use **Precise** when faces or text must stay exactly as filmed; **Creative** to restore texture in landscapes and older footage, accepting that it invents some detail.
4. Compare before and after at 100% zoom before replacing the original.

## FAQ

**Can AI restore a VHS tape?** It sharpens the picture after digitising, but tracking lines,
noise and colour bleed remain; those need restoration tools first.
